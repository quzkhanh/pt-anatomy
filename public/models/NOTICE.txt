body.glb — 3D muscle model used by PT Anatomy

Derived from Z-Anatomy (https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy),
which is based on BodyParts3D (The Database Center for Life Science, https://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/).
Changes: selected muscles only, right side only, decimated, added a smoothed head mesh built
from the Z-Anatomy head skin regions, fixed swapped trapezius part names.
