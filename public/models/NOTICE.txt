body.glb, bones.glb — 3D muscle and bone models used by PT Anatomy

Derived from Z-Anatomy (https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy),
which is based on BodyParts3D (The Database Center for Life Science, https://lifesciencedb.jp/bp3d/).
Changes: selected muscles only, right side only, decimated, added a smoothed head mesh built
from the Z-Anatomy head skin regions, added a thin neutral midline filler between the left and
right front-wall muscles, fixed swapped trapezius part names.
bones.glb: bones below the skull only (no skull, teeth, ear ossicles, laryngeal or nasal
cartilage), right side and midline only, decimated.
